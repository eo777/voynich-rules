How this works
  • Bars and numbers: rates per 10k in that section.
  • Grey band: rate kept in both sections.
  • Colored line: mass assigned in one joint maximum-flow allocation.
  • Long dashes: a two-step rule within one gap.
  • Hairline: an available pairing assigned zero in this allocation.
  • Every gap is a separate comparison. Routes can vary between equally good allocations; this is not the fitted LC1 policy.

How this works
  • Candidates: the word and its rule partners, single steps and chains, both directions. A leading q is set aside.
  • Weight of keeping: the rate kept in both sections.
  • Weight of a partner: the smaller of the word’s fall and the partner’s rise.
  • Novel: no partner weight, into keeps ≤ 50%, and a transition with G² ≥ 10.83 and ≥ 4×.
  • Transitions use Zattera units and the glyph-matrix app’s statistics.
  • A reading aid from section frequencies, not a decipherment.
